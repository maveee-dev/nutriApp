# NutriApp Clinical Policy Inventory

Status: planning and implementation roadmap

Last reviewed: 2026-08-22

This inventory records the intended clinical and nutrition-policy scope for NutriApp. It is based on the current policy registrations, evidence providers, evaluator semantics, and the guideline sources currently recorded in policy provenance.

The inventory is not itself a clinical prescription. A policy may be implemented only after its activation evidence, target semantics, freshness requirements, and provenance requirements are defined.

## Policy classifications

- **Universal**: a population reference or upper limit that can be applied without patient-specific laboratory evidence, while still allowing condition-specific precedence.
- **Individualized**: requires an approved user-specific target or clinical evidence before it can affect compatibility or adherence.
- **Contribution-only**: reported as nutrition information and progress context, but not interpreted as a food-level safety judgment.
- **Clinical-judgment dependent**: requires a clinician decision, structured clinical assessment, predicate, or interaction model that NutriApp does not currently reproduce.

A nutrient can have more than one role. For example, potassium and phosphorus are always useful contributions when canonical Food data is present, but they become compatibility constraints only when an approved individualized upper-limit rule is resolved.

## Shared activation and provenance rules

1. Universal policies may activate from their registered population reference and condition applicability.
2. Individualized policies require an approved target with a nutrient key, unit, effective date, expiry, source, version, and approval metadata.
3. Laboratory-dependent policies must preserve the test code, normalized value, unit, collection time, evidence source, and freshness decision.
4. Missing, stale, conflicting, or unsupported evidence must produce an explicit deferral. It must never become implicit approval.
5. Contribution data may remain visible when a compatibility rule is active. The same measured value must feed both views.
6. Meal Assessment and Daily Adherence consume resolved rules and immutable contributions; they do not calculate nutrient values or resolve policy evidence.
7. Planner, Recommendations, Consultation, and AI explanation remain downstream consumers and must not introduce clinical reasoning.
8. Historical snapshots must preserve the resolved target/rule, provenance, evidence identifiers, evaluator version, policy-set fingerprint, and evaluation fingerprint.

## Recommended implementation order

1. Resolve and document nutrient-specific freshness for potassium and phosphorus; remove the provisional 365-day assumption only after clinical review.
2. Complete the fluid guideline and canonical water-data audit.
3. Implement fluid only if an approved individualized target and reliable contribution semantics can be represented without inference.
4. Complete generic NumericConstraintRule migration for remaining current upper-limit policies such as saturated fat and added sugar. Cholesterol is contribution-only in current evaluations; preserve its v1 rule only for historical replay.
5. Complete generic daily progress support for fiber and energy where the semantics are clinically appropriate.
6. Reassess calcium, anemia, lipid, gout, allergy, and medication policies individually; do not activate them merely because the database contains related nutrients.
